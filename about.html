About Mohammad

A camera professional working across the needs of the set.

Mohammad Hmayed is a cinematographer and camera professional based in Lebanon. His work spans narrative productions, television series, documentaries, commercials, branded content, talking heads and podcasts.

He works in several defined camera roles, including cinematographer, camera operator, focus puller and Ronin operator. That range gives him a practical understanding of how visual choices, movement, focus and camera department coordination meet during production.

His selected work includes cinematography for the short film تجلّي (Tajalli), camera operating on شامبليون (Champollion), focus pulling on مولانا (Mawlana) and other television and commercial productions, Ronin operating, documentary filmmaking and videography for brands such as Foma Home.

Alongside production work, Mohammad has also led hands on Ronin training, sharing practical setup and operating experience with developing camera operators.

Clear about the role. Focused on the shot.

Good camera work depends on preparation, communication and attention once the camera rolls. I want the brief, the role and the responsibilities to be clear before the shoot. On set, I stay attentive to the frame, the movement, the performance and the department around me.

02Training

Hands on Ronin training

Mohammad has led practical Ronin workshops for developing camera operators, covering balancing, setup and operating technique on real rigs. The training work reflects the same priority as the production work: operators who understand the tool serve the scene better.
